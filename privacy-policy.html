Privacy Policy

MZ Conversion Booster Blocks

This Privacy Policy explains how MZ Conversion Booster Blocks handles merchant information when the app is installed and used on a Shopify store.

App Name MZ Conversion Booster Blocks
Developer Zahid Jami
Last Updated July 25, 2026

1. Introduction

MZ Conversion Booster Blocks is a Shopify app that helps merchants add conversion-focused storefront blocks, sections, trust badges, urgency bars, popups, and product page elements through the Shopify Theme Editor.

We respect merchant privacy and only collect the limited information required to install, operate, and provide access to the app.

2. Information We Collect

When a merchant installs or uses MZ Conversion Booster Blocks, the app may collect and store the following information:

  • Shop domain, such as your Shopify store URL.
  • Shopify app installation and session information.
  • Selected app plan or plan access level.
  • Basic technical information required to authenticate and operate the app.
We do not collect customer payment card information, and we do not sell merchant or customer data.

3. How We Use Information

The information collected by the app is used for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate the merchant and keep the app connected to Shopify.
  • To provide access to the correct Free, Standard, or Pro plan features.
  • To allow merchants to access app pages such as Dashboard, Blocks Library, Pricing, Setup Guide, and Support.
  • To support app functionality and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To comply with Shopify platform requirements and applicable privacy obligations.

4. Customer Data

MZ Conversion Booster Blocks is primarily a storefront design and conversion block app. The app does not require access to customer payment details, customer financial information, or sensitive customer records.

Some app blocks, such as email popup templates, may allow merchants to display email capture elements on their storefront. Any use of such blocks should be handled by the merchant according to their own privacy policy and applicable laws.

5. Shopify Data and Permissions

The app uses Shopify authentication and app installation/session data so merchants can access the app inside Shopify Admin. The app may also use Shopify theme app extension functionality so merchants can add and customize blocks from the Shopify Theme Editor.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade merchant data. We may share limited data only when required:

  • To operate the app through trusted hosting or infrastructure providers.
  • To comply with legal obligations or Shopify platform requirements.
  • To protect the security, rights, and integrity of the app.

7. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect app data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of online transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain app installation and plan access data only as long as necessary to provide the app service, support merchants, comply with legal obligations, and maintain app functionality.

When a merchant uninstalls the app or Shopify sends a required data deletion request, we process the request according to Shopify’s privacy and compliance webhook requirements.

9. Merchant Rights

Merchants may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with their store, subject to applicable law and Shopify platform requirements.

10. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party infrastructure services for hosting, database storage, and app operation. These services are used only to support the functionality and availability of the app.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
